**Novedu is a platform for AI-supported learning.** It is not a general-purpose chatbot: the teacher decides the content, the limits and the feedback. An AI such as ChatGPT turns the teacher's material into tutors, quizzes, writing tasks and coding activities, and students open them in the browser via a link.

Novedu is free and open source, made at [HTBLA Leonding](https://www.htl-leonding.at) for schools in Austria and Europe.
